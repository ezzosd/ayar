Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ayar (ayargo.com) handles information when you visit the website and use its pages and tools. We aim to limit the data we need and use it only for the purposes described below.

1. Information We May Collect

Hosting servers or usage-measurement tools may record technical information automatically sent by your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, pages visited, visit time and referral source. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website, its performance and user experience.
  • To understand visits and general use of pages and tools.
  • To detect errors and misuse and help protect the website.
  • To respond to enquiries and messages we receive.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use cookies or local storage to remember display preferences or enable certain features. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling them may prevent some features from working as expected.

4. Advertising, Google Services and Third Parties

The website currently uses advertising code and services provided by the Monetag network, including a service associated with browser notifications. Ads or promotional messages may appear depending on the settings of these services. Monetag and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and may process technical data such as IP address, browser information and interaction data needed to deliver and measure advertising. You can manage website notification permissions in your browser settings. For more information, please visit Monetag and review its policies.

If Google AdSense or other Google advertising services are enabled on the website, Google and third-party advertising providers may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or learn about choices for opting out of certain personalized ads at YourAdChoices. You can also read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We may also rely on other third-party providers for charts, analytics, data or website features. When these services load, the provider may receive technical data from your browser. Its handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Gold Price Data

Ayar displays price data and market information that may be obtained from external sources or data providers. Using the website tools does not require you to submit personal information to us, although browser requests to data providers may include technical information needed to establish the connection.

6. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information that we receive through user correspondence. Technical data may be processed by hosting, analytics, advertising and other service providers on which the website relies. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the website’s rights and security.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above or comply with legal obligations. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. External Links

Ayar pages may contain links to websites that we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their policies before using them.

9. Children’s Privacy

The website and its services are intended for general use, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10. Your Rights and Contact

You may contact us with questions about information you have sent to us or to request its correction or deletion, where possible and subject to legal obligations. Email us at info@ayargo.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, the services we use or legal requirements. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
